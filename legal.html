We want you to be satisfied with your advertising purchase. This policy explains when and how you can request a refund for paid advertising slots on aimodelranks.live.

Summary: Full refund if we reject your submission. 50% refund within 48 hours of activation if unused. No refund after 48 hours once the ad slot is live and delivering impressions.

1. Eligibility for Refunds

Full Refund (100%) — you are entitled to a full refund if:

  • Your submission is rejected by our content review team for violating our ad content guidelines
  • Technical issues on our platform prevent your ad from being displayed for more than 24 consecutive hours during your paid period
  • You were charged incorrectly or a duplicate charge occurred
  • You request a cancellation within 24 hours of purchase and before your ad goes live

Partial Refund (50%) — may be granted if:

  • You request a refund within 48 hours of your ad going live
  • You have not received significant traffic or impressions (fewer than 100 views)
  • Your circumstances have materially changed (product launch cancelled, business closed)

No Refund — refunds will not be issued if:

  • More than 48 hours have passed since your ad went live
  • Your ad has received substantial impressions (100+ views)
